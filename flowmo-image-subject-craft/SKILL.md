# Image subject craft

Graphics/typography: establish a grid, visual hierarchy, exact-copy list, font roles, brand palette and crop/safe areas. Verify every word, alignment and contrast at delivery size. For editable layouts use native design elements and generate only needed visual assets.

People/identity: establish reference roles, face/body proportions, pose, expression, wardrobe, light direction and context. Preserve the requested identity through compatible image inputs; do not infer that any reference-capable model guarantees identity. Review hands, teeth, eyes, anatomy, fabric and realistic contact with props.

Product/packaging: record the actual silhouette, scale, materials, labels, logo placement and reference viewpoints. Distinguish preservation from redesign. Review reflection, contact shadow, perspective, geometry and exact labeling. Use product-photoshoot modes where appropriate, not for every graphic asset.

Environment/anchors: specify spatial geometry, lens, material, light motivation and continuity constraints. A later video start frame needs a composable image with space for separate titles and room for the intended camera action.

Illustration: define shape language, line/texture, palette roles, perspective and the series consistency rule. Editing: identify what changes and what must stay pixel/identity consistent; verify actual edit/reference limits.

Draft intent means bounded exploration at an appropriate resolution/latency/cost, not a universal cheap model. Final output needs inspected reference fidelity, legibility and format. GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2 are product preferences; keep other compatible families eligible and validate task-specific quality before claiming a winner.
