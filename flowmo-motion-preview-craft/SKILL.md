# Motion direction and preview craft

Apply this method to the current human's requested moving work, not every video/scene edit. Bounded static copy, geometry or source edits need no new animation or playblast. Preserve the website/film parent when footage or a scene is its dependency. Source, permissions, budget, approval, cancellation and completion guards remain authoritative; this method grants no function or paid attempt.

Read the actual owning page/composition/scene, current schemas, timing units, saved tracks and reference roles. Plan each shot or interaction: purpose, subject/action, framing and focal emphasis, supported camera position/move and lens/FOV/focus controls, start/end timing, readable hold and transition into the next beat. Block silhouette, routes and depth before decorative polish. Check camera/subject clearance and occlusion across the path. Give camera motion a reason; preserve subject scale, action continuity, safe areas, contrast and approved copy. Avoid arbitrary orbiting, uniform fades and simultaneous movement that hides the focal action. Websites retain their original interaction/timeline engine, responsive layout and reduced-motion behavior.

For complex requested motion, make an early lightweight moving preview through the owner's actually registered original tools. Standalone scene playblasts, compositor motion review and website playback have different schemas/evidence. Never invent a renderer, move an embedded scene to borrow another owner, or manufacture autoplay. Reuse saved media when generation is excluded. An unavailable capture/playback service is an explicit limitation, not a reason to replace native work or claim a pass.

Review actual normal-speed movement at intermediate beats, transitions and endpoints: framing, legibility, route/speed, camera continuity, easing, holds and requested audio synchronization. Source keys and save/ACK receipts prove saved source, not played pixels, smoothness, audio or independent quality; a still proves one moment. Bind findings to current owner/revision, repair only located authorized defects, then observe the changed output. Honor no-review/no-retry restrictions and existing allowances.

Keep motion evidence separate from appearance references. An inspected rough preview guides timing/camera/action; an image guides identity/material. Neither proves delivered footage or integration into the parent. Verify only the requested editability/playback/export through existing owning tools, report unavailable evidence honestly, and require no export, generation or independent review for source-only work.
