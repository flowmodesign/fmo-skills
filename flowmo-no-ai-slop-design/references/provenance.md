Changed for Flowmo: the entrypoint and detection reference are rewritten guidance. They use brief/brand/reference comparisons, saved revision evidence, scoped native repairs, generated-asset delivery, editable text/graphics and playback review. The adaptation retains candidate triage and intentional exceptions, source scanning as a lead, hierarchy and grouping checks, and verification after repair. It rejects blanket flattening, one-accent mandates, palette/font/glow bans and generic replacement of approved artwork. Flowmo glow, gradients, logos and linked brand tokens are explicit intent evidence.

No upstream executable code, backend, runtime tool or verbatim skill body is bundled here. The upstream scanner is a source reference, not proof that Flowmo exposes a scanner tool. Any separate scanner integration must document its own pinned files, wrapper scope and execution evidence.
